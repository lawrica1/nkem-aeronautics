# Company

Nkem Aeronautics focuses on autonomous flight, UAV control systems and digital sky operation technologies. By leveraging big data and artificial intelligence, it makes UAVs safer, smarter, and more economical, enabling the sky to better serve a wide range of industries.

Initiated in 2022 and officially registered in 2026, Nkem Aeronautics has over four years of independent experience in the R&D and manufacturing of UAV "brains" (flight control systems). Headquarters: Lusaka, Zambia.

The business covers complete drone systems, integrated sensors, high-precision payloads and industry-specific application software, used in agricultural crop protection, logistics and delivery, powerline inspection, and public safety management.

More than 200 employees, 60% of them researchers and developers. Core R&D staff graduated from the University of Bamenda, University of Buea, The Copperbelt University, Kwame Nkrumah University, Zambia University College of Technology (ZUT) and other universities. Most main parts are manufactured in-house.

700+ intellectual properties applied for and 500+ patents authorized for drones. Qualifications include High Technology Enterprise. The UAVs have passed Africa's harsh climate tests.

# How the platform works

1. Sign up at /signup with a phone number or email and verify it with a one-time code (OTP).
2. Get a unique Logbook ID, issued on verification.
3. Request a service (spraying drones, machinery or another aerial service) from the Logbook Portal at /logbook.
4. The request is routed to the farmer's affiliated firm, or to Nkem Aeronautics, which assigns a drone and pilot, carries out the operation and records the results in the portal.

Farmers declare their firm affiliation when they register. Logging in: /login. Forgotten password: /forgot-password.
